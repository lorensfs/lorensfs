## 💡 About Me

In my latest years, I've learned that while I truly enjoy diving into technical challenges, **code is a means to an end**; the real goal is developing things that people will actually use and that deliver tangible value.

I'm passionate about building well-architected systems and automating processes wherever possible. I try to stay ahead by constantly challenging myself to learn new patterns and be more comprehensive in my solutions.

**Teaching and learning** are at the core of my approach. I believe in sharing knowledge.

My expertise primarily lies in **backend development, data, infrastructure, and DevOps**. Although I'm flexible enough to contribute in other areas, including frontend work when needed.
